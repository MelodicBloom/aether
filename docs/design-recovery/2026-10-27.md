## Repository-specific records

### D16 — AETHER: a developer product built around adoption confidence

**Use when:** When developing AETHER's gallery, React/Next shader package, preset taxonomy, documentation, tiering, or commercial positioning.

[Recovered intent] AETHER targets React/Next developers who want luminous iridescent surfaces without authoring GLSL. Its arc is discovery → confidence: gallery previews attract, a detail surface demonstrates controls, technical information makes integration understandable, and access/licensing is clear.

[Design DNA] Calm, precise, premium; shader previews lead. Thin-Film, Fresnel, Bubble, Shell, and Holographic were proposed behavior families. Shallow tilt, restrained sheen, quick contextual navigation, and consistent glow/depth tokens support evaluation without competing with the specimen.

[Observed boundary] The inspected repository is largely definition/handoff material, not a mature paid runtime package. A product doc's paid compare mode and freemium conversion model are design choices, not purchase validation. Optical family names must match the actual mechanism or be labeled appearance-inspired.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Define a versioned preset/renderer contract, importable runtime, supported framework/version matrix, static and reduced-motion fallbacks, clear source licenses, and an accessible inspection/comparison view. Reuse qualified mechanisms from Shader Gallery or Wildfire rather than forking untracked copies.

[Reimagined horizon] A curated material developer product with recipes, documented controls, evidence labels, integration examples, and coherent free/paid value. Keep commercial packaging separate from research authority.

[Carry-forward test] A fresh consumer project imports one preset, changes its declared parameters, handles missing GPU/reduced motion, and receives matching source/license/evidence information. Only then expand the catalog or checkout complexity.
